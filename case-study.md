# AI Trading Research Pipeline

## P — Problem & constraints

Create a structured crypto-market research workflow spanning data acquisition, exploratory analysis, sequence modelling and reinforcement-learning experimentation while preserving reproducibility boundaries.

## A — Architecture & decisions

Historical OHLCV feeds the private notebook pipeline. The retained project includes LSTM artifacts and PPO training/checkpoint artifacts for selected symbols, together with TensorBoard logs.

## C — Contribution & delivery

The private repository contains the research environment, data-acquisition utility, notebook workflow and trained-model evidence. Repository consolidation later removed ambiguity between two duplicate GitHub copies.

## E — Evidence & outcomes

Evidence verifies that the research pipeline and serialized model/training artifacts were produced. It does not establish production trading readiness or profitable future performance.

## Confidentiality

Public proof is documentation-only and excludes raw data, model files, notebooks and credentials.
