# AI Trading Research Pipeline

## P — Problem & constraints

Document a completed market-research workflow spanning historical data acquisition, exploratory preprocessing, model experimentation and reproducibility while keeping raw datasets and model artifacts private.

## A — Architecture & decisions

The private research repository contains a Python/Jupyter workflow, historical OHLCV acquisition, sequence-model artifacts, reinforcement-learning checkpoints, TensorBoard experiment logs and a pinned dependency environment.

## C — Contribution & delivery

OSYSTIC retained the data-acquisition utility, notebook workflow, experiment artifacts, training evidence and dependency environment in one canonical private repository.

- Structured the research flow from historical data acquisition through preprocessing and experiment execution.
- Preserved both sequence-model and reinforcement-learning evidence in the private repository.
- Retained checkpoint/final artifacts and experiment logs for traceability.
- Maintained a pinned Python environment for reproducibility.
- Kept raw datasets, notebooks and model artifacts outside the public showcase.
