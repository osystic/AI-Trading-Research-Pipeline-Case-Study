# AI Trading Research Pipeline — Engineering Case Study

> **OSYSTIC ENGINEERING CASE STUDY · PUBLIC SHOWCASE · SANITIZED · PORTFOLIO-SAFE**

This case study documents a cryptocurrency ML/reinforcement-learning research pipeline. It contains no private notebooks, raw datasets, model weights, account credentials or claims of guaranteed profitability.

## Problem & constraints

The project explored a repeatable workflow for acquiring historical crypto market data, preparing research datasets, training sequence models and reinforcement-learning agents, and retaining experiment artifacts for selected liquid symbols.

## Architecture & decisions

The private evidence shows:

- historical OHLCV acquisition
- notebook-based exploration/preprocessing
- LSTM model artifacts
- PPO reinforcement-learning checkpoints/final artifacts
- TensorBoard experiment logs
- a pinned Python ML environment

## Contribution & delivery

OSYSTIC retained the completed research workflow and model/training evidence in a private repository and later consolidated an older duplicate repository into one canonical source of truth.

## Evidence & outcomes

The repository supports claims about implemented research capability and artifact production. It does **not** support a public claim of profitable live trading, future return, production execution quality or risk-adjusted alpha.

## Confidentiality

Raw datasets, notebooks, model weights, logs and any private delivery context remain excluded from the public artifact.
